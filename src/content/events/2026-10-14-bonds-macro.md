We went over bonds and how they react to big macroeconomic events like Fed rate hikes, inflation reports and shifts in economic outlook. We covered why bond prices move opposite to yields, why longer-duration bonds take a bigger hit when rates rise, and how markets often price in expected moves before they actually happen. We also worked through how to graph these relationships, plotting yield curves and price/yield charts to see what a steepening or inverting curve looks like in real data.
